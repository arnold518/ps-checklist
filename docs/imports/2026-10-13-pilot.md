# First raw catalog publication

Four rows from `raw/contests.csv` were fetched, reviewed against
`raw/audit/resource-checks.csv`, and published to the local beta database.
Their problem titles and native order matched the audit exactly.

| Raw row | Contest | Published ID | Problems | Archived selected files |

The World Finals contest kept the official statement booklet and editorial from
the CSV. Two additional QOJ contest attachments were removed from the selected
material list. Its 12 selected QOJ problem statement PDFs were downloaded with
the configured QOJ session and archived. The raw ordered official statement
PDFs replaced fetched statement links for both IOI days; the raw official
statement and editorial PDFs replaced fetched links for KOI High.

One fetched QOJ discussion for IOI Day 1's World Map was removed: it was a
community post rather than an organizer-published editorial. The per-contest
refresh policy records that removal. No fetched warnings or unresolved problem
matches remained at publication.

The public catalog now contains seven contests. A sample archived PDF from
each new contest was served through `/api/archive/<id>`. A local database and
archive backup was taken immediately before and after publication. Fetch and
prepared payload details are retained only in ignored
`storage/output/pilot-fetch-20261013/` on this machine.

New rows still require individual CSV paste and review in the admin page.
This pilot does not establish that the remaining raw rows can be published
without decisions about source quality, missing files, or problem matches.

The published pilot records were subsequently deleted during the full catalog
reset described in [the full fetch log](2026-10-13-full-fetch.md). The table
above records the completed pilot, not the current database contents.
